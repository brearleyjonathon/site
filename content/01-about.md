# About

Jon Brearley lives in Brooklyn and works as an associate at [Transsolar KlimaEngineering](https://transsolar.com/) in New York, where he focuses on climate-responsive design for projects from Santa Cruz to Saudi Arabia. He is a [Certified Passive House Designer](https://passivehouse.com/en/training/certificates/designer-consultant/), speaks at conferences on passive design, serves as a critic at architecture studio reviews, and sits on the content committee for [NESEA BuildingEnergy Boston 2027](https://nesea.org/conference/buildingenergy-boston-2027).

Previously, Jon worked as a research affiliate at MIT's [Urban Risk Lab](https://urbanrisklab.org/), where he made visualizations for Miho Mazereeuw's book [*Design Before Disaster*](https://urbanrisklab.org/dbdbook/) and contributed to MIT's Climate Grand Challenges. Before that, at [MIT's Leventhal Center for Advanced Urbanism](https://lcau.mit.edu/), he conducted research on heat-resilient housing in Kuwait City and co-authored two peer-reviewed papers on the work. This work and other publications are on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mlDAczYAAAAJ&hl=en).

Jon studied architecture at Portland State University before starting at MIT, where he earned a [Master of Architecture](https://catalog.mit.edu/degree-charts/master-architecture) and a [Master of Science in Building Technology](https://architecture.mit.edu/node/159). His joint thesis, [*Taming Torridity*](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/5f7bce7d-d3af-446f-ba32-aa5894b6b914), explored heat resilience in the American single-family home. He received the Tucker-Voss Award and the Faculty Leadership Award upon graduating.

During his time at MIT he co-chaired the [IDSA Boston](https://www.idsa.org/communities/chapter/boston/) chapter with [Sheng-Hung Lee](https://www.shenghunglee.com/) and MIT's [Architecture Student Council](https://architecture.mit.edu/student-groups) with [Ginevra D'Agostino](https://liminalfutures.com/ginevra-dagostino-eng). He was also a member of the design and planning collective [Group Project](http://groupproject.us/index.html#info).

Off the clock he dabbles in running, flying drones, taking photos, and starting side projects he may or may not finish.
