# About

Jon Brearley lives in Brooklyn and works as an associate at [Transsolar KlimaEngineering](https://transsolar.com/) in New York. He focuses on climate-responsive design for projects from Santa Cruz to Saudi Arabia.

Previously, Jon was a research affiliate at MIT's [Urban Risk Lab](https://urbanrisklab.org/), where he made visualizations for Miho Mazereeuw's book [*Design Before Disaster*](https://urbanrisklab.org/dbdbook/) and contributed to MIT's Climate Grand Challenges. Before that, at MIT's Leventhal Center for Advanced Urbanism, he conducted research on heat-resilient housing in Kuwait City and co-authored two peer-reviewed papers on the work. His publications are on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=mlDAczYAAAAJ&hl=en).

Jon studied architecture at Portland State University before heading to MIT, where he earned a [Master of Architecture](https://catalog.mit.edu/degree-charts/master-architecture) and a [Master of Science in Building Technology](https://architecture.mit.edu/node/159) and received the Tucker-Voss Award. Off the clock he dabbles in running, flying drones, taking photos, and starting side projects he may or may not finish.
