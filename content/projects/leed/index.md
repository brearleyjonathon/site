Every prerequisite and credit in the July 2026 edition of LEED v5 BD+C gets a page of its own. Each one shows the intent, the points for New Construction and Core and Shell, and every option drawn as a line of stations. Alongside that are the numbers worth memorizing, the standards each credit runs on, and the credits it shares work with.

I made it while studying for the LEED AP BD+C exam. It is a study aid, so check anything that matters against the rating system itself.
